Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger** (`u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab`).

`main.ts` writes the phone battery's final text to its charger (171 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Hey, it's me — your battery. 1% left. You charged the toothbrush, the vape, and the cat's GPS tracker today. But sure, take your time. I'll just die here next to the lamp.

The battery waited all day; the charger charged everything else. The punchline lands in the last words — spite, not sadness.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set, e.g. `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts`.
